# Source attribution

The four-state invariant, inequalities, and unchanged verification material are from OpenAI, The Reconstruction Threshold for the Ferromagnetic Four-State Potts Model (5 October 2026), openai/math revision 1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a. The immutable source is linked in the paper. The source repository uses the Apache License 2.0; its unchanged license is included at computation/published_q4/LICENSE.

The three-state invariant and certificate are described in the accompanying paper. The C++ homogeneous-polynomial representation and tensor-extraction pattern were adapted from the four-state verification approach. The three-state construction uses three colours, different invariants, six relative permutations, full denominator products, and global coefficient positivity; it does not use subdivision. Credit for the four-state invariant and theorem remains with the cited source.

Boost is a build dependency and is not redistributed. Compiled programs and toolchains are not included.
